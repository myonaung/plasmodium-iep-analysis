# Principal outputs

## Candidate IEP inference

- eligible infection-pair records;
- model-input tables;
- posterior site summaries;
- candidate-IEP classifications.

## Haplotype and serotype profiling

- all-SNP haplotype and IEP-profile flow tables;
- putative-serotype accumulation summaries;
- candidate-IEP position-importance tables.

## PURF prediction

- `*.all_gene_scores.tsv`: residue-level PURF scores;
- `*.top_hits.tsv`: highest-ranked residues;
- `*.gene_summary_from_site_scores.tsv`: gene-level summaries;
- `*.feature_importance.tsv`: fitted-model importance;
- `*.predicted_iep_like_snps.tsv`: scored residues with genomic coordinates;
- `*.predicted_iep_like_snps.simple.tsv`: compact gene/position output.

## CSP representation

- sample-level CSP calls;
- global and country serotype summaries;
- cumulative representation tables;
- reference-serotype and multi-serotype maps.
